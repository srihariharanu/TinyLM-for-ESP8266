# Community code of conduct

Treat everyone respectfully. Welcome beginners and constructive disagreement.
Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, personal attacks, spam and disclosure
of private information are unacceptable in project issues, reviews or discussions.

Report concerns privately to Sri Hariharan U at srihariharanu@gmail.com.
The maintainer may request edits, remove content, close discussions or restrict
participation to address harm. Reports should be handled discreetly, without
retaliation, sharing only what is necessary to address the issue.
