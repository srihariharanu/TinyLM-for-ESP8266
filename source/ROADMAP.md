# Future updates

Experimental; designed for ESP8266 with 4 MB flash. Future updates are planned,
without promised delivery dates or support SLA.

- Broaden and clean training examples and publish larger held-out evaluations.
- Reduce repetitive replies and improve unfamiliar-input handling.
- Explore smaller models and faster integer inference within ESP8266 RAM limits.
- Improve accessibility, connection recovery and flashing experience.
- Validate more boards and operating systems.
- Strengthen automated release checks and reproducible build metadata.

Suggestions and contributions welcome. Proposed work is not a current feature.
