# Security policy

The latest experimental release is considered for fixes; there is no patch SLA
or guaranteed response time. Intended for trusted local research demonstrations.
See docs/SECURITY_AND_PRIVACY.md for the design's known limitations.

Do not publish exploitable details, private credentials or tokens in public
issues. Email srihariharanu@gmail.com with version, impact and a minimal
reproduction. Coordinate disclosure after the maintainer has had an opportunity
to review. Only test devices and networks you own or have permission to test.

Rights contact: **srihariharanu@gmail.com**.
